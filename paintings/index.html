Marking The Fish

Theme Inbetween
Time Taken 35 hours
Medium Acrylic on Paper
Year 2026
Dimensions 80.5 × 60.5 cm
Work Type Unique Original Work (1 of 1)
Status Available for acquisition

Fish Painting treats contemporary art as a way of using different techniques to bring a subject to life, rather than following a single style. Traditional pattern-making meets bold, unapologetic colour across the body of the fish, with a tiny painting within the larger one waiting for those who zoom in. It is a celebration of Indian maximalism, patterns and colour, and of Indian contemporary art's ability to hold the old and the new at once.

Foliage

Theme Inbetween
Time Taken 75 hours
Medium Acrylic on paper
Year 2021
Dimensions 79 × 58.6 cm
Work Type Unique Original Work (1 of 1)
Status Available for acquisition

Forest Painting is less about painting nature and more about painting light moving through it. Hours of work went into discovering that nature has its own unique shades and knows how to balance itself perfectly-imperfectly. Look closely and the greens split into entirely different shades, tones and textures.

Studio Process · Painting

Form, Rhythm & Detail

Working with acrylic on paper, each artwork develops through an organic balance of initial composition, thoughtful color layering, and detailed surface work.

01
Initial Composition:

The piece begins with an exploratory drawing, mapping out composition, rhythm, and form across the surface.

02
Color & Layering:

Colors are mixed and built up in progressive layers, allowing depth, texture, and relationship between forms to emerge.

03
Detail & Refinement:

Intricate motifs and finer elements are added patiently by hand, bringing the artwork to its final, unified expression.
