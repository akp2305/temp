Selected Works & Prints

✦ Studio Standards

Archival Craftsmanship

Every impression is treated with the highest museum-grade respect.

100% Cotton Rag

Acid-free Somerset Velvet (UK) and handmade Indian Khadi rag. Deckled natural paper edges with rich tactile tooth.

Lightfast Oil Inks

Artist-grade Charbonnel & Cranfield Caligo relief inks. Deep, non-fading pigments formulated to endure for generations.

Finite Editions

Strict small runs of 6 to 8 impressions. Each print is hand-titled, numbered, and signed in archival graphite pencil.

Plastic-Free Transit

Acid-free glassine wrapping and heavy corrugated backing boards. Insured safe delivery across all states in India.
