✦ Project Formats

Commission Specialties

Four enduring ways people invite hand-carved relief art into their lives.

House & Heritage Portraits

Hand-carved architectural portraits of family homes, childhood dwellings, or historic facades. Emphasizing brick textures, rooflines, and garden foliage.

Personalized Ex-Libris Bookplates

Classic bookplates designed for avid readers and private libraries. Numbered suites printed on lightweight Japanese washi or adhesive archival paper.

Botanical Monograms

Intricate intertwined letters framed by native botanicals, ferns, and local flora. Perfect for personal correspondence cards, crests, and framed gifts.

Milestone Keepsake Suites

Artworks commemorating marriages, anniversaries, or studio milestones. Includes framed prints for the hosts and numbered suite impressions for guests.

✦ The Journey

How a Commission Works

A transparent, personal collaboration from first sketch to final impression.

01

Consultation & Photos

We begin with a conversation over WhatsApp or email discussing your vision, reference photographs, dimensions, and preferred paper stock.

02

Mirrored Composition Sketch

I create an archival graphite pencil sketch drawn in reverse. We review the light, shadow contrast, and balance together until approved.

03

Studio Carving Updates

I hand-carve the linoleum block using Japanese gouges. You receive photo and video clips from the studio as the cuts take shape.

04

Printing & Plate Handover

Your edition is hand-pressed on 250gsm Somerset cotton rag, signed, and carefully packaged. You receive both the printed suite and the original carved plate.
