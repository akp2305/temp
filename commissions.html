✦ The Journey

How a Commission Works

A transparent, thoughtful collaboration from first conversation to final installation.

01

Consultation

We begin with a conversation exploring your space, vision, reference themes, scale, and preferred medium.

02

Research & Concept

Contextual research, mood explorations, and preliminary conceptual sketches tailored specifically to your setting.

03

Development

Detailed composition drafts, tonal balance studies, and palette choices reviewed collaboratively before studio execution.

04

Finalisation

Hand-carving the relief plate or layering canvas strokes in the studio, with progress dispatches shared as the artwork takes shape.

05

Delivery & Installation

Careful archival packing, signing, certification of authenticity, and safe nationwide delivery or site placement.
