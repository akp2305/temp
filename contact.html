Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about packaging, shipping, framing, payment, and print care.

How are the prints packaged for delivery? +
Every print is wrapped in acid-free glassine tissue and sandwiched between two heavy 4mm corrugated rigid boards inside a water-resistant mailer. Larger works are safely rolled into heavy-walled cardboard postal tubes. We use 100% plastic-free, recyclable packaging materials.
What does "Limited Edition" mean? +
When an artwork is marked "Edition of 8", it means a finite edition of eight numbered impressions are hand-pulled from the relief block. Each individual print is hand-inspected, editioned, and signed in pencil by the artist (for example, 2/8).
What paper and inks do you use? +
We exclusively use archival, museum-grade fine art papers including 250gsm Somerset Velvet (100% cotton rag made at St Cuthberts Mill in England) and genuine 300gsm Indian Khadi handmade rag papers. Our inks are professional artist-grade Cranfield Caligo and Charbonnel oil-based relief inks renowned for deep, lightfast pigmentation that will not fade for generations.
Do prints come framed? +
To keep shipping safe and affordable across India, all prints are sold unframed. However, all our pieces are designed around standard frame sizes (e.g. 10×10", 11×14", 12×16", A4, A3). We recommend framing under UV-protective glass with an acid-free mat board that allows the deckled paper edges to breathe.
How should I care for my original linocut? +
Hang your framed artwork away from prolonged, direct harsh sunlight and damp areas (like unventilated bathrooms). Never touch the inked surface with bare fingers; handle by the edges or wear clean cotton gloves.
What payment methods are supported? +
We accept direct UPI transfers (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) with zero gateway commissions, direct bank transfers (NEFT/IMPS), as well as credit/debit cards via secure payment links upon request.
What is your exchange or return policy? +
In the extremely rare event that your package arrives damaged in transit, simply take a photo within 48 hours of delivery and message us on WhatsApp. We will immediately arrange a replacement or full refund without hassle.
