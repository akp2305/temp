✦ Curatorial Notes

The Dialogue with Texture

How the rhythm of relief cutting translates onto the tactile surface of raw linen.

Ground & Grain

Unlike primed white canvas that reflects light flatly, raw Belgian linen absorbs and refracts ambient studio light. Each linen weave has natural irregularities that anchor the pigment, creating a surface that feels alive and grounded.

Repetition as Meditation

Where printmaking uses repeated gouge strokes to clear a block, the paintings use deliberate mark-making to build volume. Small geometric intervals repeat across the plane, creating microspaces of stillness within large compositions.
