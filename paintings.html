Fish Painting

Theme Contemporary art · Indian Maximalism
Year 2026
Medium Acrylic & mixed media on canvas
Dimensions 80.5 × 60.5 cm
Work Type Unique Original Work (1 of 1)
Status Available for acquisition

Fish Painting treats contemporary art as a way of using different techniques to bring a subject to life, rather than following a single style. Traditional pattern-making meets bold, unapologetic colour across the body of the fish, with a tiny painting within the larger one waiting for those who zoom in. It is a celebration of Indian maximalism, patterns and colour, and of Indian contemporary art's ability to hold the old and the new at once.

Forest Painting

Theme Contemporary art · Light & Foliage
Year 2021
Medium Acrylic & mixed media on canvas
Dimensions 79 × 58.6 cm
Work Type Unique Original Work (1 of 1)
Status Available for acquisition

Forest Painting is less about painting nature and more about painting light moving through it. Hours of work went into discovering that nature has its own unique shades and knows how to balance itself perfectly-imperfectly. Look closely and the greens split into entirely different shades, tones and textures.

Studio Process · Works on Canvas

Building Surface, Chromatic Rhythm & Maximalist Detail

Working across acrylic and mixed media on canvas, each painting develops through an intuitive dialogue between monochromatic value drawing, fearless pigment application, and delicate surface ornamentation.

01
Value Structure & Underpainting:

The composition begins with a tonal monochromatic underdrawing, mapping out spatial rhythm, form, and shadow before colour enters the work.

02
Chromatic Rhythms & Layering:

Saturated vermilion, ochre, and jewel-tone pigments are applied in deliberate layers, allowing underlying values to vibrate through vibrant fields of colour.

03
Micro-Ornamentation & Indian Maximalism:

Intricate motifs, rhythmic patterns, and micro-details are painted by hand with fine brushes, creating hidden worlds within the larger subject that reveal themselves on close inspection.
